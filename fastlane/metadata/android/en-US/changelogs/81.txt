Fixed: a clock nobody had touched said it followed one particular published set of prayer-time numbers. Religious settings showed a set as chosen whenever the numbers in force happened to match it, and the app's own defaults — dawn and dusk at 18° — are the numbers Karachi publishes, so a new install showed that set and wrote its names beside the app's own. Nobody had chosen it. The set is now the one that was pressed, and it is Custom until somebody chooses one.

Also fixed: the line under today's times on Sunset/sunrise settings said 18° whatever set was chosen, and now writes the numbers actually in force; and a set whose angle is half a degree (19.5°, 17.7°) left the angle row with nothing lit, as if no angle were set, so the angle in force now has a chip of its own.
