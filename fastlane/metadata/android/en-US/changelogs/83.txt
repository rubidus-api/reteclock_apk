Added: a screen orientation setting on General settings — follow the phone (the default, and what the clock always did), follow the sensor, lying down, or standing up. For a screen that cannot be turned, or a device with no rotation sensor at all: a reader or a tablet on a wall. It turns the clock; the settings pages and the screensaver follow the phone as before (issue #61).

Added: a choice of how a tap reads the time, on General settings, Tapping the clock. As written — "1:05" — or numbers only — "1 5", with no colon and no leading zero. Some speech engines read a written time character by character, "zero one colon zero five", and two bare numbers are what those read as a time. As written is still the default, and your AM/PM word follows the numbers either way (issue #62).
