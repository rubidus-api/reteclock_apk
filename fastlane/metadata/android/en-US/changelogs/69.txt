Added: a bell can be put off. Give a bell a number of minutes in Sounds and bells, and
when it rings while the clock is on screen a card comes up with it: ring again in that
many minutes, or stop. Back is stop, and a card nobody answers closes itself after a
minute. Bells you already set are untouched — they have no number until you give them
one. A bell rings while the clock or the screensaver is showing and not otherwise, so
a bell put off is forgotten if you leave the clock.

Added: how good animations may look is now a setting. Kind to the phone is the default
and exactly what the app always did; Better makes frames the size of the screen and
stores them as WebP instead of raw pixels. A step is not saved when it is chosen — the
phone bakes the hardest picture you use, plays it timing every frame, and the setting
is written only if that survives.

Added: a field can be taken off in the layout editor. Tap it on the layout, then
Remove; Undo is beside it.

Fixed: the layout editor drew the shared pool's background while the clock drew the
picture the layout itself carries, so a layout with its own pictures was arranged
against one background and shown against another.
