New: the published sets of numbers the sun's day can be read by, on Religious settings — Muslim World League, ISNA, the Egyptian General Authority of Survey, Umm al-Qura, Karachi, Tehran's Institute of Geophysics, and Shia Ithna-Ashari, beside Custom.

Choosing a set fills in the numbers, and the numbers are written out on the page. Changing any of them makes the choice Custom again. They are carried for convenience, not as a ruling: check them once against a timetable you trust.

With a set chosen, the name it uses for each moment is shown beside the app's own — "Dusk · Isha" — and there is a switch to turn that off. Three reckonings came with the sets: dusk as a fixed interval after the evening, an evening a few degrees below the horizon rather than at sunset, and a night that ends at dawn. The evening is an event a bell can follow now.
