A more dependable timer. One background service plays every cue for the whole run, screen on or off, and an exact alarm wakes the phone for each: no count-in heard twice, no cues lost to Doze, a battery saver or a closed app. Timer sounds play on the alarm volume, from the speaker even with a headset connected; on vibrate it buzzes, on silent it only shows, and a notification says it finished. Timer settings: Battery saving (Android 6+), with a button to its settings.
