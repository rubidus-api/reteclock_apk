* Fixed: with Android's high-contrast text switched on, the clock was outlined
  whatever you had chosen and a picture used to fill the writing did not appear.
  The clock now draws its writing as shapes while that setting is on, and the
  settings say the setting is on and what it does elsewhere.
* A phone on silent now stays silent: no sound on vibrate, and neither sound nor
  vibration on silent — bells included.
* A timer set to vibrate or to silent says nothing and plays none of your sounds.
* Pictures can be brought in several at a time, and the Pictures page points at
  the zip that carries a whole folder.
